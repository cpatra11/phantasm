# Surface: basix app (board, editor, auth screens)

Visitor mode: Operate. Small marketers and founders complete a task: brand URL to storyboard to export.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The construction grid is the product. Every card, layer, and scene snaps to a visible cell grid, the way a designer lays out a Crouwel poster, and the grid stays on screen as part of the tool. It refuses the category default of a floating dark panel with glowing gradients.

OWN-WORLD: Off-white ground with faint blue construction hairlines on a 12-column cell grid. Ink-black text in a single grotesk. One saturated accent, cobalt blue, used only for selection, the primary action, and the active layer. Monumental letterforms appear only in the empty state and the sign-in headline, built from the same grid cells.

STORY: The visitor understands that basix places their brand into a grid of scenes. They believe every change lands in a known cell. They act by typing a brand URL, writing a prompt, selecting a card, and exporting.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left column, one cell wide: product mark, then the navigation (Board, Brands, Exports, Credits). Main area: the board, with the brand card and prompt bar as the top row of grid cells, followed by three storyboard cards snapped to the grid. The primary action, Generate, sits at the right edge of the prompt bar. Credits remaining sit top right, always visible.

FORM: Operate-mode app shell on a visible 12-column grid, with the Crouwel display treatment limited to the empty state. Position on the ordered list: direction roll, assigned index 7 (Crouwel grid specimen, chosen by the user). Seed key 0ff9bdc3.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
